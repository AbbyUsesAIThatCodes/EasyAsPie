## Input Contract

A plate gesture owns one pointer and the plate on which it starts. Empty origin latches ADD; occupied origin latches ERASE. Units traversed by the shortest step between pointer samples are accumulated, including wrapping over the origin. Purple previews add; red previews cover every traversed unit even when empty. Release fills missing or removes existing units only. Leaving the plate pauses collection; release outside commits collected units. Pointer cancellation, Escape, lost capture, blur, hidden tab, mode/task/reset cancel uncommitted previews. A background-origin gesture owns the camera until release and never paints. Other pointers are ignored while one owns a gesture.

Native piece buttons support clicks/taps and roving arrows/Home/End. Enter/Space toggles an individual unit. Shift+arrows starts/extends a latched keyboard preview; Enter commits; Escape cancels. Clear and Undo affect construction only, never erase committed history. CUT PIES commits the entire active order; editing and hovering never submit. Free Play keeps its distinct Cut/Regroup operations.

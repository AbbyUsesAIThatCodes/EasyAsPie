## Misconceptions To Address

- **Bigger denominator means more pie:** divide the same whole more finely while preserving the selected amount.
- **Same numerator means same amount:** compare 1/2 and 1/4 of equal wholes.
- **Add the same number to top and bottom:** demonstrate multiplying or dividing both by the same factor.
- **Every sixteenth simplifies:** 3/16 stays 3/16; only whole-slice equivalents in the allowed denominators are accepted.
- **Equal fractions always mean equal physical quantities:** explicitly keep both whole pies the same size.
